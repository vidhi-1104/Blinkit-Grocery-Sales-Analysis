# Blinkit-Grocery-Sales-Analysis
Retail and sales performance dashboard for Blinkit (quick-commerce) analyzing outlet types, location tiers, and product categories to track revenue distribution.

# 🛒 Blinkit Last-Minute Grocery Sales Analysis

## 🎯 Business Context
Operating in the highly competitive quick-commerce market requires precise monitoring of inventory performance and geometric expansion. This dashboard tracks **1.20M in Total Sales** across 141 outlets to break down revenue by regional tiers, establishment history, and item categories.

## 💡 Key Analytical Insights
* **Revenue Hotspots:** Tier 3 locations generate the largest share of sales (39.29% / $472K), demonstrating massive market validation outside primary city hubs.
* **Core Drivers:** Everyday essentials—led by Fruits & Vegetables and Snacks—dominate the item volume rankings, validating Blinkit's "last-minute grocery" core value proposition.
* **Operational Channels:** Supermarket Type 1 is the primary fulfillment workhorse, delivering $787K of the total revenue, while maintaining a consistent 4-star average customer rating.

## 🛠️ Technical Toolkit & Features
* **Power BI Desktop:** Implemented custom corporate color thematic branding (Blinkit Yellow/Green).
* **Multi-Page Interaction:** Utilized cross-filtering buttons to toggle views seamlessly between Total Sales, Average Sales, and Item Counts.
* **Data Modeling:** Combined multiple store dimensions (Location, Establishment Year, Fat Content) into a unified relational star schema.
